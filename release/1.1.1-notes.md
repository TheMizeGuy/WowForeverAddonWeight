First release on CurseForge, for World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.x).

Addon Weight shows how heavy each of your other addons is. `/weight` opens the window.

- Four tiles name the heaviest addon on frame rate, stutters, memory and loading.
- A sortable list gives every loaded addon's average and in-combat frame time, worst frame, hitches, memory and load time, and says in words why an addon stands out.
- Two tabs: this session, and all time. The all-time figures are saved across reloads and sessions.
- Figures of addons you remove go at the next login, unless you turn on the setting that keeps them.
- Right-click a row to disable that addon for this character and reload.
- Memory is only scanned on a timer when a scan takes under 10 ms; a slower scan runs while a loading screen is up or when you open the window, so the meter does not cause hitches of its own.
- The meter reads each addon in a frame of its own, about one profiler call per frame with 60 addons and the window closed.
